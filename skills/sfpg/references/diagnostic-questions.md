# SFPG diagnostic questions

Ask these of the team, role or agent where the problem shows up. A "don't know" is itself a finding.

## Structural: who owns what

- What is this unit responsible for?
- What is outside its scope?
- What inputs does it receive, and what outputs does it produce?
- What systems and data can it access?
- Which person owns accountability for the outcome?
- When must it escalate, and to whom?
- Which other units does it work with?

Principle: separation of concerns. Avoid one giant role, or one "do everything" agent.

## Functional: what can be done, and how

- What specific task does this capability perform?
- When should it be used?
- What inputs does it need?
- What quality standard must the result meet?

Principle: capabilities are reusable and written down, not held in one person's head.

## Procedural: how work moves

- **Empower:** did whoever does the work get enough context, tools, authority and boundaries?
- **Delegate:** was the work routed to the right person, team, agent or system?
- **Follow up:** did anyone check that the work reached the intended outcome?

Details in [procedural.md](procedural.md).

## Governance: what must be controlled

- Which actions are allowed without review?
- Which require review, and by whom?
- Which are prohibited?
- How are exceptions escalated, and how is the work audited?
